To meet our 5-day minimum trading requirement, a day is only validated if it contains at least one trade held open for 3 minutes or more.
  • The Intent: This rule prevents "flick-trading" (opening/closing in seconds) just to pass the time requirements.
  • Validation: As long as one trade meets the 3-minute threshold, that entire day counts as active, regardless of any other shorter trades you place.
  • In Profit: If you haven't reached the 5-day minimum or met the consistency threshold, your cycle simply extends. You can continue trading until all criteria are satisfied.
  • In Loss: If the account is in a loss at the end of the cycle, you must continue trading until you return to profit to request a payout, provided no maximum loss limits were breached.

An International Bank Account Number (IBAN) is a standardized global code used to identify bank accounts across national borders. It ensures cross-border payments are processed efficiently and reduces the risk of errors.
Important: Fewpips primarily processes payments through secure Payment Service Providers (PSPs) and Cryptocurrency. If bank transfers are available in your region, the IBAN will be provided directly on your deposit invoice during checkout.
  • Account Number: Used internally by a specific bank within a single country.
  • IBAN: An international format that includes country code, bank identifier, check digits, and the account number for global transfers.
IBAN length depends on the country, typically ranging from 14 to 34 characters. The most common format is between 20 and 24 characters, and each country follows a fixed structure.
